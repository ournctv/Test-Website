About the Nantucket Golf Club Foundation:

Through our Grant and Scholarship Programs, the Nantucket Golf Club Foundation supports island nonprofits serving children and young people and provides scholarships to Nantucket High School graduates pursuing post-secondary educational opportunities.

NGCF Vocational Scholarship Program Documentary (2023)

This documentary profiles the Nantucket Golf Club Foundation’s Vocational Scholarship Program, now called the “Professional Scholarship Program”. This program provides renewable scholarships to students who have exhibited a demonstrated interest in a professional program and plan to pursue further education and technical training to gain employment in their chosen field. Applicants are evaluated based on scholarship, character, and career experience and goals. This program was introduced in 2018. Students awarded this scholarship have gone on to pursue degrees in nursing, education, welding, automotive technology, and more.
