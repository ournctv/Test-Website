About Nantucket Public Schools:

Nantucket Public Schools (NPS) serves the unique needs of the island community of Nantucket, Massachusetts. Committed to academic excellence and student success, the district includes five schools: Nantucket Elementary School, Nantucket Intermediate School, Cyrus Peirce Middle School, Nantucket High School, and Nantucket Community School. As a department of the Town of Nantucket, NPS operates under state education laws and regulations set by the Massachusetts Department of Education.

By fostering an educational community where all learners feel a sense of belonging, experience growth and joy, and are empowered to positively impact the community, Nantucket High School creates an equitable, inclusive, and challenging learning environment that prepares students to excel after high school and embody the characteristics of our Vision of a Graduate.
