About the Rossini Club:

The Rossini Club creates unique concert experiences that bring together chamber music with food, art, theater, and storytelling. We are committed to offering welcoming and interactive performances while showcasing artists of the highest caliber. Drawing from the rich arts tradition of Nantucket, we present music that is rarely heard alongside beloved masterpieces. Through thoughtful and evocative programming, we strive to create experiences that inspire, challenge, and celebrate the vitality of the arts within our community.

Dreamland Live Sessions: The Rossini Club (S01, E07)

THE ROSSINI CLUB is a collective of chamber and classical musicians led by Nick Davies on the clarinet and Ivy Ringel on the bassoon. THE ROSSINI CLUB has performed on island each summer since they were formed in 2012. Dreamland Live Sessions is a weekly, winter film series highlighting the myriad talents of our many island musicians, bringing these artists together with fans in a safe and innovative way.

Find out more about these amazing artists in the episode interview or online at www.therossiniclub.org! When on Nantucket, Nick and Ivy are also involved at the Nantucket Community Music Center. Additionally, all episodes of Dreamland Live Sessions are mixed at the NCMC.

Special thanks to Jamie Howarth of Plangent Processes for mastering the audio on this episode of Dreamland Live Sessions.

A Tribute to Ned Rorem

The Rossini Club pays tribute to honorary Nantucket composer, Ned Rorem, who passed away in late 2022. The composer, known for both his art songs and lurid diaries, lived on the island for many decades and wrote music about or dedicated to the island. Two of his works, the lush Nantucket Songs and the diary-like Winter Pages, will be performed on this concert. To complement these works, two pieces by Hungarian composers György Ligeti and Béla Bartók fill out the program.

The Edge of Waking at the Nantucket Dreamland

Courtesy of Nantucket Dreamland, experience the emotions of music both liminal and ecstatic in this unique program of early Romantic music, featuring Schumann and Mendelssohn in contrast with their 20th century counterparts. For their debut performance at the Dreamland, Rossini Club directors Nick Davies and Ivy Ringel will perform alongside longtime collaborator Wesley Ducote in this tribute to the artfully expressive Austro-German lyric tradition.
