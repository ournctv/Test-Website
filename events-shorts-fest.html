Short films inspired by Nantucket since 2013.
The Nantucket Shorts Festival celebrates short-form movies that are 2 to 10 minutes long, inspired by the island of Nantucket. Its unique format allows both experienced and amateur filmmakers to present their art on the big screen at the Dreamland Theater in an entertaining evening unlike any other. A jury screens each submitted film and selects the top films to appear at this festival.

Guidelines

How to Submit to the 2027 Shorts Fest

Film Submission Criteria at a Glance:

  • Films must be created on or inspired by the island of Nantucket.
  • Film length: 2-10 minutes.
  • Films cannot be a commercial for a local business.
  • Films must have a beginning, middle, and an end.
  • Submission fee is $50. Student & first-time filmmakers: $35.
  • Top films will be selected by a jury and shown at the Dreamland Theater.
  • All music must have music rights and be appropriately cleared.
  • Submission format: 1080p /24p/ h.264
  • Films may be posted online to a pay-per-view link as part of a fundraiser benefitting NCTV. This link will be active for no greater than 1 year.
  • Films may be shown a few times at the Dreamland during “encore” presentations of the Shorts Fest.
  • All films will be shown on NCTV Channel 8 & 1072.
  • All films will be shown on NCTV's YouTube Channel.
  • Submissions are due by 5pm on 9/10/27.
  • Questions? Email the Shorts Fest Director Lisa Getter at lisa@ournctv.org
