Nantucket Climate Change Summit

Climate Change Summit: Winter Edition (Part 1) – The State of Climate Change on Nantucket

A webinar in the winter run of the Nantucket Climate Change Summit, the series the Foundation and the Linda Loring Nature Foundation began the previous September. Dr. Jen Karberg and Dr. Sarah Bois give a presentation they had delivered in several versions before, recorded here so it could be kept. They begin with the global picture, using ice and soil cores to show the pattern in atmospheric carbon, and a temperature map on which New England stands out as warming faster than most of the world. The local material follows: what warming water means for the island’s shellfisheries, commercially and as a community fishery; flood maps for higher tides and heavier rainfall; erosion around the coast; species arriving from further south, usually carried in landscaping material, which have not yet caused serious trouble here; and phenology, meaning the timing of leaf-out, flowering, fruiting and caterpillar hatch. They close on the organizations working together under the island’s biodiversity initiative, and take questions.

2025 Nantucket Climate Change Summit

The third annual Nantucket Climate Change Summit, presented by the Foundation and the Linda Loring Nature Foundation and recorded by NCTV. The theme is local change: what climate change means for Nantucket in particular rather than in the abstract. The hosts note the event has sold out every year it has run. The global framing comes first, with a warming map on which New England stands out, and the rate of global sea level rise. The local material covers where the water will go as tides rise and rainfall intensifies, erosion around the island, and the roughly four percent more moisture the atmosphere holds for every degree Fahrenheit of warming. One speaker explains why climate models give a range rather than a figure: the atmosphere is easier to predict than human behavior. The later part turns to what people can do, and to polling on how many Americans are concerned about climate change set against how far they feel their governments act on it. Funded by the Great Harbor Yacht Club Foundation and the Community Foundation for Nantucket’s Remain Nantucket Fund.

2026 Nantucket Climate Change Summit

The 4th Annual Nantucket Climate Change Summit presented by the Nantucket Conservation Foundation and Linda Loring Nature Foundation. This year’s summit focuses on community-driven resilience. The topics include salt marsh restoration at Long Pond, the Massachusetts coastal flood risk model, the water-level sensors and community science project, how monitoring gets sustained financially, and how outreach reaches the residents most exposed to coastal hazards.
