Island Feud: A Dreamland Game Show – 5/13/2023

It’s time to play The Island Feud! May 13th, 2023 marked the first ever Dreamland Game Show, live on Nantucket Dreamland’s Main Stage, and we are thrilled to be bringing more games to the stage, including ‘Jeoparty’ January 27th, and the second ‘Island Feud’ May 18th!

Featuring local teams ‘Nantucket Bake Shop’, ‘Snowballs Trivia Masters’, ‘The Bookclub Babes’ and ‘Team Chicken & Salad’, the first Island Feud was a showdown for the ages.

Brought to you in part by The Beet, Nantaco, Nantucket Crisps, Hyline Cruises & Nantucket Quarterboards.
Hosted by Ty Fleischut
