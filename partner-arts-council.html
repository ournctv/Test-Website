About the Nantucket Arts Council:

The Nantucket Arts Council is a fiscal sponsorship fund and a scholarship fund under the Community Foundation of Nantucket, that supports and encourages a collaborative community of the visual, performing and literary arts through education, grants, and scholarships.

The Emergence Series

The Nantucket Arts Council and Nantucket Community Television present a series of 4 episodes featuring interviews with 24 Nantucket artists about their work during the pandemic. All of the featured artists received grants from the Nantucket Arts Council to encourage them to continue their work throughout this challenging time in celebration of the arts.
