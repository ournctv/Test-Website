Nantucket Land Council - 2nd Annual State of the Harbor - 7/19/18

Executive director Cormac Collier opens the second annual forum, and the head of the town’s sewer department presents on the sewer main break of the previous winter and the steps being taken to improve the system where it bears on the harbor. A panel then takes questions across the rest of the picture — water-quality testing and monitoring, nitrogen and nutrient loading, stormwater and fertilizer runoff, eelgrass and shellfish.

Nantucket Land Council - 3rd Annual State of the Harbor - 7/18/19

The third annual forum, newly co-sponsored by the Great Harbor Yacht Club Foundation, whose scientific advisory board contributes a talk on the harbor’s past, present and future drawn from two decades studying eelgrass along the Cape. The Land Council’s executive director frames the work in the organization’s 45th year — the sole-source aquifer, the wetlands and salt marshes, the freshwater ponds and estuaries — before the program turns to nutrient and nitrogen loading, algae, eelgrass and shellfish, and the monitoring behind the numbers.

Nantucket Land Council’s State of the Harbor 2021 Forum: Green Boating for a Healthy Harbor

Nantucket Land Council’s 2021 State of the Harbor Forum is a culmination of their Green Boating for a Healthy Harbor campaign with a presentation and discussion around boat use in our harbor, the effects on our resources and infrastructure, and how we can all participate in preventing or mitigating these impacts on a personal and community level. Nantucket Land Council’s 2022 State of the Harbor Forum: Planning for a More Sustainable Future

Nantucket Land Council’s 2022 State of the Harbor Forum serves as an introduction to the Town of Nantucket’s process for updating the 2009 Nantucket and Madaket Municipal Harbors Plan. With an update on the state of Nantucket Harbor water quality and ecosystem health, the Nantucket Natural Resources Department explains the purpose of the Harbor Plan and how the Nantucket community can contribute to discussions on local regulations, policies and practices around harbor uses and the preservation of the harbor environment.

Nantucket Land & Water Council’s 2025 State of the Harbor Forum

The Nantucket Land & Water Council is pleased to present its 9th Annual State of the Harbor Forum to provide the latest information on issues impacting the health of Nantucket Harbor. The presenters provide updates and reflections on the status of our eelgrass, discuss success and challenges with restoration efforts in Nantucket Harbor, and present the Town of Nantucket’s first Eelgrass Management Plan with thanks to the Great Harbor Yacht Club Foundation for supporting this important effort. The health of Nantucket’s eelgrass habitat requires collaboration and commitment from the entire Nantucket community to first protect what we have, and to continue to use research, monitoring, and restoration tools for long-term management and sustainability.

Nantucket Land and Water Council’s 2026 State of the Harbor Forum

Join the Nantucket Land and Water Council (NLWC) and speaker Shannon Hulst, Floodplain & Community Rating System Specialist for the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institute Sea Grant Program and Cape Cod Cooperative Extension, in an engaging dialogue about coastal resilience policy. Ms. Hulst reviews several provisions of a model bylaw for Nantucket to consider incorporating into its local regulatory framework, and seeks community input on them. As the Town implements its Coastal Resilience Plan, the NLWC is looking for your opinion on how we build resilience into our harbor and surrounding properties and landscapes Laura Marrett, Principal from Scape Landscape Architecture DPC, follows with a brief presentation outlining the Town of Nantucket’s new Strategic Retreat and Relocation Program. The Town is in the process of gathering community input as they design this program, and will be hosting in person and/or online opportunities for additional input later this summer.

Introducing the Nantucket Land Council’s Clean Water - Topics on Tap

The Nantucket Land Council is excited to present their newest virtual educational series: Clean Water – Topics on Tap!
